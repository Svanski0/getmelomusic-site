FAQ

Is melo free?

Yes. melo is free on iPhone. melo+ removes ads, lifts the saved-audio limit and unlocks extras like custom colors.

Is melo on Android?

Android testing is coming soon. Get it on Android →

How do I save an audio?

On any TikTok, tap Share → melo. Or open melo and paste a link. You can also import audio from Photos or Files.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once an audio is saved, it lives on your phone and plays offline, including from your lock screen.
