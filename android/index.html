Install in a minute

  1. Tap Download melo 1.0.7 on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser for this one time.
  3. Tap Update or Install. Your library stays on your phone when updating a matching melo install.

Updates

Using the older Sandbox build? Download the Sandbox update to keep your current library. Switching from Sandbox to the production app requires a fresh install. Keep your existing app installed until your library is backed up.

melo checks for new versions when you open the app and while you use it. When an update is ready, it appears in melo. Android asks you to confirm the install.

Already installed? Open Settings → Check for updates to check now. The APK is verified before Android opens its installer.
