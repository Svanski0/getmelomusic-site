Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information melo ("the app", "we") collects, how it's used, and your choices. melo lets you save audio from links you share into the app or from files on your device, then loop, trim and organize it into playlists.

Information we don't collect

melo does not require an account. We don't ask for your name, phone number, contacts or precise location.

Content you save

Audio, covers, trims and playlists you create are stored on your device. Imports from Photos or Files are processed entirely on your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too, except for any device backups you control through your phone's settings.

Links you import

When you share or paste a link into melo, the link is sent to our server, which retrieves the media so the app can extract the audio. To make imports faster and more reliable, our server may store a processed copy of the audio and cover for a given public video so repeat requests for the same video load quickly. This stored copy is not linked to your identity. We keep basic, short-lived server logs (such as request time, the link requested and technical error details) to run and secure the service and fix bugs.

Advertising

The free version of melo shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking when asked), IP address, approximate location derived from your IP, device and app information, and ad interactions. If you decline tracking, you may still see non-personalized ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent where required, and you can change your choice in the app's settings. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. melo+ subscribers don't see ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple (and, on Android, by Google Play when available). We don't see or store your payment details. The store tells the app whether you have an active melo+ purchase.

Bug reports and support

If you contact us or submit a bug report, we receive the information you choose to send (for example your email address, message and device model) and use it only to respond and fix issues. Our bug form is hosted by Tally.

Children

melo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us and we'll help. You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings.

Changes

If we change how melo handles data, we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
